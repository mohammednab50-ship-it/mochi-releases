🍡 Mochi — Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026 · Applies to the Mochi apps for Android and Windows.

Mochi is a study companion for medical entrance exams (NEET PG). This page explains what Mochi keeps about you, why, who helps run it, and how to delete it. We don't sell your data, show ads, or use advertising or analytics trackers.

What Mochi keeps, and why

WhatWhy
Account: your email address (and your Google name if you sign in with Google)To sign you in and keep your progress in sync between your phone and PC.
Profile: your name, exam and year, optional username, optional profile pictureTo greet you, pick questions for your exam, and let friends find you.
Study data: decks and flashcards, practice questions and your answers, review schedule, study plans, focus sessions, doubts you askTo run the app: reviews, practice, plans and your stats, synced across your devices.
Friends: friend requests, study rooms, live "studying now" status, cheersTo study together with friends you add. Only your friends and room-mates see your status and picture.
AI requests: the text (and any photo you choose) you send to Mochi's AI featuresTo answer you. It's sent to our AI provider to produce the answer. We keep only a record of each request's size, cost and which feature it was, not its content.
Usage statistics: on which days you used Mochi, the app version, how many times each screen was opened (just the screen's name), and crash reportsTo see which features help, and to fix crashes. Never what you typed or studied.
Bug reports: your note, the screen name, app version, and a screenshot if you include oneTo fix problems you report.
Voice (viva practice)Turned into text by your phone's speech service when you tap the microphone. Mochi doesn't record or store audio.

Who helps run Mochi

  • Supabase stores your account and synced data (our database and server functions).
  • Anthropic (Claude) processes AI requests to produce answers. Anthropic doesn't use API requests to train its models.
  • Google handles "Continue with Google" sign-in, if you use it.

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS). Each person can only read their own data; friends see only what's described above.

Your choices

  • Delete your account any time: in the app, Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and everything stored with it on our server. You can also ask us by email (see Delete your Mochi account).
  • Sign out any time; what's on your device stays there until you uninstall the app.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, or to correct it, by email.

Children

Mochi is made for medical students and graduates. It isn't meant for children under 18.

Medical content

Mochi is a study aid. Its AI can make mistakes, and nothing in it is medical advice for patients.
