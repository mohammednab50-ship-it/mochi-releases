🍡 Delete your Mochi account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Mochi and go to Settings.
  2. Under Account & sync, tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted straight away.

Without the app

Email mochilabspvt@gmail.com from the address you sign in with, with the subject "Delete my Mochi account". We delete it within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

Everything stored with your account on Mochi's server: your account and profile, synced decks, flashcards, questions and answers, study plans, focus history, doubts, friends, study rooms, cheers, AI usage records, bug reports and their screenshots, and payment records.

Nothing is kept afterwards, except where the law requires us to keep payment records for tax purposes (amount and date only).

Data on your own phone or PC stays there until you uninstall the app.
